## Archive layout

- `program-overview/`: program, course, and closing-overview materials.
- `pre-course/`: the delivered introductory course PDF.
- `lectures/`: the five delivered module decks.
- `labs/`: complete copies of the labs and the Online Lab Assistant used in this training.

## Maintenance policy

This archive does not synchronize with the maintained root-level `lectures/` and `labs/` directories. Later course improvements belong in those mainline directories. Update this archive only to correct an omitted or misplaced delivered file.
