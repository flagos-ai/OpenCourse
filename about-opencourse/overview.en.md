# Project Overview

FlagOS OpenCourse is an open educational project for AI systems software and
intelligent computing. It organizes heterogeneous computing, AI operators,
compiler optimization, distributed training, and performance analysis into
teaching and lab resources for higher education.

## Why AI systems software education

Progress in AI depends not only on models, but also on the systems software
that makes workloads executable, measurable, and optimizable: hardware-aware
programming models, runtimes, compilers, operator libraries, communication, and
performance engineering. Learners benefit from understanding how these layers
work together.

## What OpenCourse provides

- a five-module curriculum framework;
- module-aligned lecture materials, labs, and environment guidance;
- modular and full-course adoption paths for different teaching contexts;
- learning pathways that connect teaching, hands-on work, course projects, and
  open-source practice.

The core curriculum is 48 hours and 3 credits. A general course on AI large
language models may be offered as an optional pre-course outside the core hours.
See the [course structure](course-structure.en.md).

## Design principles

### Systems perspective

The course moves from heterogeneous hardware and programming models through
operators, compilers, distributed training, and performance measurement to give
learners a cross-layer understanding.

### Practice-driven learning

Each module connects to runnable lab materials so learners can test concepts,
record results, and reason about performance on a real software stack.

### Adaptable adoption

Institutions can integrate individual modules into existing courses or adopt the
full curriculum. Credits, assessment, delivery, and resource arrangements are
adapted to local requirements.

### Open collaboration

Course resources, labs, and technical practice connect to open-source
ecosystems and support continued learning, reproduction, and improvement.
