# Course Structure

The FlagOS OpenCourse core curriculum is a **48-hour, 3-credit** course with
five sequential modules. Institutions may adapt credits, assessment, and the
delivery term to their local curriculum requirements.

## Optional pre-course

The general course on AI large language models is an **optional three-hour
pre-course**. It provides context on foundation models and AI compute, but is
not included in the 48-hour core curriculum. Its hands-on companion is the
[Image-to-Text Inference lab](../labs/pre-course/iluvatar-corex-bi-v150/).

## Teaching materials and labs

The mainline repository maintains Chinese and English lecture materials and
module-aligned labs. For lab scripts, follow the [Labs guide](../labs/README.md)
to clone the current OpenCourse repository from GitLink. Completed training
deliveries are preserved as independent snapshots under [editions/](../editions/)
and do not automatically track mainline changes.
