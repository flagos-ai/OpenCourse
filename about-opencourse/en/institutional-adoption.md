# Institutional Adoption

Institutions may adopt individual modules, the full five-module core course, or
co-develop teaching and practice activities with FlagOS OpenCourse. The core
curriculum is 48 hours; the general course on AI large language models is an
optional three-hour pre-course. See the [course structure](../course-structure.en.md).

## Adoption paths

### Modular adoption

Select one or more modules for an existing architecture, systems, machine
learning, or compiler course.

### Full-course adoption

Use the five-module core curriculum with the corresponding teaching materials
and labs. The institution determines credits, assessment, and local scheduling.

### Co-development

Collaborate on teaching content, lab delivery, faculty development, or
education-and-research activities. Scope is agreed by the collaborating parties.

## Support

- Chinese and English teaching materials;
- hosted online lab environments and free compute-resource support;
- faculty development for China-based and international partners;
- technical exchange and course-delivery guidance.

Resource availability, free-support eligibility, quotas, and provisioning
timelines are agreed for the specific collaboration.

## Collaboration principles

- Course resources are for teaching and non-commercial educational use.
- Lab resources may be used only for approved teaching activities and learners.
- Intellectual-property ownership and permitted use are agreed by the parties.
- Sensitive information obtained during collaboration is treated confidentially.
