# Faculty Support

FlagOS OpenCourse provides faculty-development support for China-based and
international adopters. The format, timing, and scale are agreed for each
collaboration.

## Support areas

- curriculum structure and module sequencing;
- lab-environment and hands-on teaching guidance;
- use of lecture decks, assignments, labs, and course projects;
- focused training and teaching exchange.
