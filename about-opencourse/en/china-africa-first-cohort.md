# China–Africa First Cohort: A Case Study

This case study records a China–Africa faculty-development effort as presented
in the first-cohort progress materials. It is an example of institutional
adoption, not a template that every institution must follow.

## Institutional mobilization

At the University of Gondar, returning trainees escalated the training outcome
from department to college and university leadership before an institution-wide
call for applications. The reported objective was to build institutional
ownership before broadening participation.

## Training and teaching practice

The first cohort delivered a six-day hands-on program. Reported activities
included FlagOS practical orientation, model execution, peer learning, and
connections between teaching and applied AI work.

## Curriculum integration

The progress materials describe a pathway from stand-alone training to formal
curriculum integration, including partnership discussions and a planned
fourth-year, first-semester offering. Timing and local approval remain the
responsibility of the participating institution.

## Partnership context

The progress materials identify collaboration involving the University of
Gondar, AU-ASRIC, and BAAI, and note further partnership discussions. Any
future public case update should be reviewed with the participating institutions
before publication.
